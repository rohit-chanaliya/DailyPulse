Delete your DailyPulse account

DailyPulse is a mood tracking app for Android. This page lets you request deletion of your DailyPulse account and the data stored with it.

Delete from the app (fastest)

Open DailyPulse, go to Settings → Delete Account and confirm. Your account, mood entries and journal notes are deleted immediately.

Open DailyPulse on Google Play

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the email address registered on your account with the subject "Delete my DailyPulse account".

Email deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your DailyPulse sign-in account.
  • Every mood entry you logged.
  • Every journal title and note.
  • The local copy stored on your device.

Timing

In-app deletions are immediate. Email requests are processed within 30 days. Once deleted, data cannot be restored.
