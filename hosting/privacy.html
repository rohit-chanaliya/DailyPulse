DailyPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

DailyPulse is a mood tracking app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

Data we collect

Account

  • Email address, first name and last name you provide when you create an account.
  • If you sign in with Google, the name, email address and profile photo link of your Google account.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password.

Profile (all optional)

  • Gender, date of birth and location (free text you type), and the avatar you pick.
  • A profile photo you upload stays on your device only. It is never sent to our servers.

Your entries

  • Mood entries: a mood level, an optional title, an optional note, optional tags and the date and time.

Feedback

  • If you send feedback, the areas you select (for example "Performance" or "Bug"), the time, and your account ID. There is no free-text field.

Crash reports

  • If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics receives a technical report: the error and stack trace, app version, Android version, device model and a random installation ID. Reports do not include your email, name, mood entries or notes.

On your device only

  • Onboarding state, daily reminder settings (time, sound, vibration), an uploaded profile photo and feedback waiting to be sent.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device. It does not use push messages from our servers, and no data is sent to schedule it. Android may ask for notification permission.

Where data is stored

Your mood entries are stored locally on your device first and are synced to your private account in Google Cloud Firestore. Your profile details are stored in your private account in Cloud Firestore. Only your account can read your entries and profile. Feedback can be written but not read back by the app. Data in transit is encrypted. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In, and crash reports by Firebase Crashlytics. These Google services process data on our behalf.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The only diagnostic tool we use is Firebase Crashlytics, to find and fix crashes.
  • Mood notes are never shown to anyone else.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Feedback contains no personal text and is kept after account deletion, but it is no longer linked to any account.

Children

DailyPulse is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not a medical service

DailyPulse is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or emergency support. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a qualified professional.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the app (Settings → Delete Account), or request deletion from our account deletion page. This removes your sign-in account, mood entries, profile details and the local copy of your entries. Uninstalling the app removes anything else stored on your device, such as an uploaded profile photo.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date at the top.
