DailyPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

DailyPulse is a mood tracking app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why it is collected, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

Data we collect

  • Email address and name you provide when you create an account.
  • Mood entries: a mood level, an optional title and an optional note.
  • App preferences such as your theme choice and onboarding state, stored on your device.

Where data is stored

Your mood entries are stored locally on your device first and are synced to your private account in Google Cloud Firestore. Data in transit is encrypted. Account authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Mood notes are never shown to anyone else.

Children

DailyPulse is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not a medical service

DailyPulse is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or emergency support. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a qualified professional.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app (Settings → Delete Account), or request deletion from our account deletion page.
